## Clock (realistic purchasing)

Daily VCP (6-12 week zigzag contractions + volume dry-up + depth <= 25%), 52-week proximity, AVWAP, and ATR use **completed daily** bars through the prior session.

- **Buy:** first RTH 15m **close** above the frozen pivot and AVWAP with 15m RVOL >= 1.5 (`signal-close`). Last RTH bar allowed. Not a same-session daily lookback.
- **Sell:** ATR*1 hard stop (strategy default, not channel-touch k=2 clamp) or weekly close below the 10-week SMA, fill the **next** RTH 15m mid (overnight OK). AVWAP loss of control may raise the stop. Entry 15m is skipped. Not the daily occupancy clip. Partial 25% take-profit was not modeled (full position only).
