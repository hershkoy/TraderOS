Last-15m H2 is buying a same-session resist chase; in a Fed-hike bear (2022) and a choppy/narrow 2023 those prints fail the next day and tag ATR k=2.

Monthly: almost every 2022 month is red (Jun n=1, Jul flat, Aug small green). 2023 is mixed — May / Oct / Nov bounce months around failed-breakout clusters (Feb–Mar, Sep 0% WR, Dec). Geometry (channel_pos, formation, ATR%) is not different enough to be the cause.

Volume-delta / doji / failed-breakout overlays never flipped this bucket because they do not change the entry: they clip some losers and some fat winners. 2022's losers are fast hard-stops, often gaps.

Unrealistic 1d clip / next-mid books **are** green in 2022-23 (next-mid unique +1.62 / 1.56). That is lookback, not this live last-15m clock.
