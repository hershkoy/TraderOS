# Last-15m wheel instead of stock (Theta + Polygon) — 2026-10-09

**Verdict: mid looks resilient through 2022–23 vs same-row stock; ask/bid fails 2022 and is ~flat on 2022–23 — no promote.**

Hypothesis: on `current_best` last-15m H2 fills, sell a one-cycle wheel (CSP → assign → CC) instead of buying stock.

## Rules

- Signal: `channel_touch_1d_last_15m.csv`.
- Expanded sample: **n=500**, ADV≥$2M, buy≥2022-01-01, **ensure all 2022+2023** then fill from later years (seed=42) → attempted years `{2022:99, 2023:223, 2024:85, 2025:51, 2026:42}`.
- Open ~30 DTE / ~30Δ CSP; hold to expiry; assign → CC; call assign or liquidate shares.
- P&L: cash-secured % = pnl_per_share / put_strike × 100.
- Marks: Theta EOD mid and ask/bid. Occupancy not re-walked.
- Stock compare: same filled rows' stock `gain_pct`.

Code: `utils/research/last_15m_wheel.py`, runner `scripts/research/claimcheck_last_15m_wheel.py`, tests `tests/unit/test_last_15m_wheel.py`.
