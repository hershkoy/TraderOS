# Deepvue weekend routine (Richard Moglen)

Notes from [How I find A+ Swing Trades - Full Pro Trader Weekend Trading Routine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-3pARNMerQ) (Deepvue, 2026-08-23). Auto-captions were triplicated from auto-dub; this is a cleaned process note, not a transcript dump.

This is **not** a promote, not a live/nightly book, and not a weekly-chart drop-in for channel-touch. It is a discretionary growth/swing weekend workflow: index first, then themes, then a tight list.

**Chart defaults he uses:** 21 EMA, 50 SMA, 200 SMA (swing preset). Weekly 10 / 40 when on weekly. RMV for tightness. He prefers bars over candles.

## Flow

1. Index ETFs (QQQ is enough if time is short)
2. Theme tracker (existing vs developing)
3. Mega-caps + theme-leaders list
4. One screen: Deepvue Leaders (~200–300 names)
5. Wide list (~30–40) → weekly focus (~15) → morning daily focus (~4)

## 1. Market: QQQ vs 21 EMA

If he could look at one thing: **QQQ vs a rising 21 EMA**.

| QQQ vs 21 EMA | Stance |
|---|---|
| Above a rising 21 after a real pullback/reclaim | Aggressive |
| Chopping around the 21 | Defensive: test small, listen to feedback |
| Below the 21 | Mostly cash |

Higher context: long-term uptrend = above a rising 200-day. A normal consolidation (that tape: ~55 days, ~12% deep) is not automatically a sell. Coiled MAs at a prior support/pivot is a Ross Haber **launchpad**: expansion can go either way; have a list ready, size from the decision.

He also glances at IWM, IWO, SPY. Same read: key level, market has to decide. Full-time traders: skip the rest and just watch QQQ + 21 EMA.

**Dimmer switch:** expand higher / reclaim 10–21 EMA and break the range high → put on test risk. Break the range lows → stops take you off; move toward cash. Newer traders wait for the clear uptrend.

## 2. Themes

Deepvue Theme Tracker (dashboard preset: Richard Moglen Duo Theme Tracker).

- **Right pane, 1-month:** existing leading themes. Top 5–6 is the fishing pond.
- **Left pane, daily since open:** developing. A theme that keeps showing in the top ~5–7 over ~4 days, and is **not** already a 1-month leader.
- Full-time shortcut: check weekly instead of daily, but he prefers listing the daily top 6 and starring repeats.

That weekend’s existing: gold/silver miners, genomics, Bitcoin, software, medical/biotech. Developing: cyber, growth, medical/healthcare, materials. Software was the one he wanted prepped for the next week.

**Backdrop:** software + genomics ≈ AI (code + drug research). Miners + Bitcoin ≈ macro. Themes move in waves. Miss the first burst → wait for a pullback to the **10 EMA** / next tight area; do not chase extension and do not abandon the leading theme.

Livermore / Darvas / O'Neil / Weinstein frame: leading stocks in leading groups.

## 3. Mega-caps and theme leaders

Persistent watchlist, updated slowly (weekly-ish):

- **Mega-caps:** TSLA, META, MSFT, AAPL, AMZN, GOOGL, NVDA, etc. When these coil at the 21 EMA or a gap/pivot, the whole tape often expands with them. He looks at the names, not only MAGS.
- **Theme leaders:** 2–3 liquid, institutional, still-able-to-move names per leading theme. Keep prior-cycle leaders (memory/semis that tape) while they pull back; they often set up again. Add miners/crypto reps when rotation shows (that tape: FCX, IBIT).

**RMV** (relative measured volatility): 0 = range contraction, ready to expand; 100 = already expanded. Timing tool, not the thesis. Thesis = larger base / earnings gap / trend. Execution = tight at the 21 EMA up the right side, near a base pivot, or after a gap.

Do not buy earnings in anticipation. Hold through only with ~10–15% cushion (more if ADR is high). Binary events stay off the focus list until after the print (day-1 or reconfirmation).

## 4. Screen: Deepvue Leaders

If he could run one screen for the rest of his life: **DV Leaders** (~200–300 names in trend, leading themes, momentum, CANSLIM-ish). Bookmark it.

Two jobs:

1. **Situational awareness** — the week’s big movers should confirm the theme tracker.
2. **Trade ideas** — tight spots in larger setups.

### Sorts

| When | Sort | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Weekend | % change current week | Top movers’ industries vs theme tracker |
| Daily | % change today | Same, faster rotation |
| Ideas | RMV 15-day, tightest first | His style is tightness at a level |
| After a washout day/week | DCR or WCR = 100% | Closed at highs of the day/week (relative strength) |

**DCR / WCR:** close location in that bar’s high–low range. 100% = close at the high; 20% = weak close.

### Three setups (all from tightness)

1. Tight area up the **right side of a base**
2. Tight area **after an earnings gap**
3. Tight **pullback to the 10 EMA or 21 EMA**

Goal: immediate expansion from cost. Tight in the middle of nowhere is useless.

While flipping (~300 names, spacebar; Shift+Space to flag):

- Prioritize names already on the leaders list even if the pattern is not “ready.”
- Watermark = industry so theme membership is visible without extra clicks.
- Measure a prior expansion (shift-click drag). Skip names that cannot trend.
- **ADR** ideally **> 3%**.
- **20-day average dollar volume:** ~$20M floor, **$50M+** preferred, **$100M** better. Share count is secondary (ideally >100k); dollar volume is the liquidity that institutions can actually use.
- First cut is loose. Second cut is ruthless.

## 5. Funnel

| Stage | Size | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| DV Leaders | ~200–300 | Universe |
| Wide list | ~30–40 | Theme + some tightness + leaders; not picky |
| Weekly focus | ~15 | Must look ready to expand **tomorrow**; 1–2 names per theme |
| Daily focus (open) | **~4** | Only these until ~10:30 ET, then the wider 15 |

Watching 15 names at the open produces worse execution, not more winners. Overwhelm at the open means the list is too long. He still misses names from the 15; the bet is size + tight risk on the 4.

Leaders can live in their own section and not count against the “15 ideas.”

## 6. Risk and size (his discretionary rules)

- Stop: about **2–3%**, the **day’s low at entry**.
- Time stop / profit management: **two closes below the 21 EMA**.
- Starter size (that tape): **15%**, scaled by recent trade results.
- Fast swing (e.g. compressed on the 10 EMA): treat as a swing. Slower quality name: compression near the **21 EMA**.

## 7. RS vs RMV

1. Strong names first: **1-month and 3-month RS ≥ 85, ideally 90+**. Ignore 9–12 month RS; rotation is faster and a 12-month 90 can already be in a downtrend.
2. Then wait for **low RMV** (tight area) in those names.

High RS that is still extended is not a trade. Low RMV in a weak name is not a trade.
