## Learned User Preferences

- Prefer workspace-scoped MCP config (project `.cursor/mcp.json`) over global always-on Cursor MCP for tools like TradingView
- When validating a strategy, prefer finding historical examples in TimescaleDB before live signal hunting or full-universe backtests
- Persist coverage inventories, strategy plans, and phase status under `docs/status_log/` (roll-up `current_status.md`; Weekly BigVol under `weekly_bigvol/`; edge hunt under `edge_hunt/`; channel-touch under `edge_hunt/channel_touch/`; day digests under `daily/`)
- For market-data coverage questions, focus on stocks and exclude options unless options are requested
- For long multi-symbol hunts or backtests, record wall-clock timings in the relevant status log
- For rare-signal strategies, assess with PF/expectancy/payoff/MDD per `docs/assessing_strategies.md`; do not treat win rate as the primary gate; channel-touch: unique symbol per calendar day (not RS top1 as a hard cap; `--max-entries-per-day 1` remains optional) and ATR hard-stop k≈2.0 clamped 1.5%–6% (tester −6.25% = 6% ceiling + 0.25% friction, not a 3% stop) — do not replace ATR keepers with a fixed 2% stop (PF can rise while expectancy/WR fall); soft-promote `--require-in-channel` + `--max-channel-span-days 365` + `--max-beyond-width 0.25` (0.0 too strict; 0.5/1.0 worse on IB-windowed stack); reject H3 lower-40% geometry, hard ADV/ATR floors, SPY SMA50 alone, structure-exit bundles, L3 shakeout rebuy, and ridge P&L confidence sizing on RS-top1 (nightly stays equal-dollar); prefer longer history than 2022-only windows (unwindowed full-panel scans miss early years; windowed 504/252 recovers pre-2020); treat the edge as fat-tailed (median trade can be negative while mean/PF stay positive) and stress with drop-top-N/winsor/bootstrap; portfolio max-concurrent is a live gap before capital sizing; l3_touch wick fills may only use completed bars before fill (15m `i-1` / `--feature-asof prior-bar`); do not promote fill-bar `min_close_loc` or daily `--intraday-fill 15m` until it beats wait-6/RSI-50 without the fill bar's close
- Edge-hunt promotion vs SPY is risk-adjusted: Sharpe > 1.0, Sharpe >= SPY, and MDD better than SPY (CAGR is reported, not the ranking objective)
- After Phase 5 stock-level signals and Phase 6c VIX overlays failed to clear or lift the gate, prefer freezing and productionizing Phase 6b near-KEEP `Blend_VT60_BV40` over more Sharpe>1 fishing on the same window
- Validate the BigVol backtester visually via TradingView entry/exit order markers on discrete sleeve trades before trusting vectorized fills
- Classical chart-pattern detectors must follow Edwards & Magee / Murphy / Bulkowski rules: ascending channel ≠ ascending triangle (flat top); require distinct swings with significant intervening moves, ≥2 confirmed opposite-boundary touches, and reject repeated support/resistance violations
